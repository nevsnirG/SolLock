Crypto sent to someone's phone on Christmas morning can be sold before lunch. A locked gift is different. It belongs to them from the moment you make it, and it opens on a date you choose: next Christmas, an 18th birthday, New Year's Day ten years from now. Until then nobody can touch it, the two of you included.

This is the December version of the gift guide: what to prepare, how long it takes, and what to wrap. It does not say whether Solana is a good present. Its price moves both ways, and the gift may be worth less on the day it opens than on the day you give it.

## What to get ready before Christmas

1. **The recipient's address.** A lock pays out to a Solana wallet the recipient holds themselves, such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. An address at an exchange cannot withdraw from a lock, so a gift sent there is lost. Ask for the address, or, for a surprise, make a new wallet for them and give its recovery phrase along with the gift.
2. **Solana in your own wallet.** Enough for the gift, the fee and a little for the network. If you still have to buy it, start early: an exchange can take a few days to check a new customer's identity. [Buying your first Solana and keeping it](/learn/buy-your-first-solana-and-keep-it) has the steps.
3. **Ten minutes for a test.** A [free test lock](/lock?test) of 0.01 SOL to your own wallet shows every screen the real gift goes through.

The lock itself takes a few minutes and needs no delivery, so Christmas Eve is early enough for that part. The buying is what does not work at the last minute.

## Making the gift

On [the lock page](/lock) you choose the amount and the date, say that it is a gift, and paste the recipient's address. The page checks the address, reads it back to you and shows what you are about to sign. You approve once in your wallet, and the gift exists.

The cost is SolLock's fee of 0.25% of the amount, at least 0.01 SOL, plus a network fee of a fraction of a cent. About 0.0017 SOL stays in the lock as a deposit and goes to the recipient when they withdraw.

[How to give Solana as a gift that opens on a date](/learn/give-solana-as-a-gift) goes through the address checks one by one. Read it before a gift of any size.

## Picking the date

- **Next Christmas.** One year, short enough to look forward to, and something to open together.
- **A birthday that counts.** Eighteen, twenty-one, thirty. For a child, see [Solana for your child](/learn/solana-gift-for-your-child).
- **New Year's Day** of a year that means something to the two of you.

The date cannot be changed afterwards, by anyone. The longer the lock, the more it matters that someone still has the wallet's recovery phrase when the day comes.

## What to put under the tree

A lock cannot be wrapped, so wrap its paperwork:

- **A card** that says what it is, in your own words: how much, and the date it opens.
- **The recovery note.** The success screen gives you one. It holds the lock's address, the date and the recipient's address, and explains how to withdraw with any Solana tool, in case this website is gone by then.
- **The recovery phrase,** if you made the wallet for them, in a sealed envelope of its own. Those words are the gift. Treat the envelope like cash, and tell the recipient to keep it somewhere that will still be there on the date.

On the date the recipient connects their wallet on [My locks](/my-locks). The lock shows up by itself, with a Withdraw button.

## Before you do it

- **A gift is final.** You cannot cancel it, change the date or take it back, and neither can SolLock.
- **The price can go down.** Nobody knows what the gift is worth on the day it opens. Give an amount you are comfortable with either way.
- **A lost recovery phrase is a lost gift.** Nobody can make a new one.
- **Tax.** A gift may count for gift tax where you live, and the recipient owns the locked Solana from the start. The rules differ by country. This is not tax advice.

The account behind the gift is a standard Solana stake account with a lockup, described in Solana's [stake account reference](https://solana.com/docs/references/staking/stake-accounts). It opens on the date with or without this website.
