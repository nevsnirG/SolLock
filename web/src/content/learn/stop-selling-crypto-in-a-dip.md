Most people who sell in a crash did not plan to. They had decided to hold for years, and then one red week, one headline and one open app later, the decision was gone. People call it panic selling. The problem was not what they knew. It was that the sell button was within reach at the one moment they could not be trusted with it.

A commitment device fixes that by changing what you can do, not what you want to do. Odysseus had himself tied to the mast before the sirens sang, because he knew he would not resist them once he heard them. An alarm clock across the room works the same way. So does a time lock.

## Why willpower fails at exactly the wrong time

Prices fall fastest when the news is worst, and that is when every signal around you says sell: the chart, the group chat, the notification. In that state, a plan made in a calm month feels like someone else's plan. Telling yourself to be disciplined next time assumes a calm you that is not there when it matters.

The reliable fix is to decide once, while calm, and make the decision hard to undo.

## Ways to make selling harder

Roughly from softest to firmest:

- **Delete the exchange app** and keep the wallet off your phone. Reinstalling takes ten minutes, which is sometimes enough to break the spell, and sometimes not.
- **Move the Solana to a hardware wallet** and put it somewhere inconvenient. Selling now takes an evening instead of a tap, but it still takes only you.
- **Give the recovery phrase to someone you trust** with instructions to hand it back on a date. Firm, but now you depend on their nerve, their memory and their safe.
- **Lock it on the network until a date.** Solana has a native account type with a built-in lockup. Before the date the network refuses every withdrawal, and when the lock's custodian is [an address nobody has a key for](/custodian), nobody can make an exception: not you, not a friend, not SolLock.

Only the last one takes the decision out of human hands altogether. [How to lock Solana until a date you pick](/learn/how-to-lock-solana) walks through it.

## What a lock does not do

A lock stops a sale. It does not stop a fall. Locked Solana keeps following the price, which can go down as well as up, and on the date it is worth whatever the market says that day. If you are locking because you expect the price to rise, know that nobody can promise that, and that the lock would hold just the same if it falls.

A lock also stops the sale you would have been right to make. There is no emergency exit for a lost job, a medical bill or simply a changed mind. Treat the date as a withdrawal ban, and lock only Solana you could do without for the whole time, in a bad year as much as a good one.

And a lock does nothing about the risk you already carry: a wallet with a backed-up recovery phrase is the only way back in, and staying out of the market for a time is itself a choice with consequences either way.

## Choosing an amount and a date

- **The amount** is the part you have already decided to hold, and that you would not touch in a bad year. Keep the rest where you can reach it. Many people lock a share and keep a share free, so the lock protects the long-term part without tying up everything.
- **The date** should have a reason you can say out loud: a round number of years, a child's birthday, a year you want to stop checking the price. The lock page offers one, five and ten years, New Year's Day of a few coming years, and any date up to the end of 2076. Past ten years it asks who will still have your recovery phrase then.

Start with a [free test lock](/lock?test): 0.01 SOL for about a day. You go through the whole thing, including the withdrawal, before you lock anything that matters. If the test already makes you nervous, lock less, or pick a nearer date. If it does not, [pick your date](/lock).

The account type behind all this is described in Solana's [stake account reference](https://solana.com/docs/references/staking/stake-accounts). It is the same kind used for staking, but a SolLock lock is not staked and earns nothing: [Locking vs staking Solana](/learn/solana-locking-vs-staking) has the difference.
